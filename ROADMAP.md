- **HEIC / JPEG 2000 on Windows.** Both formats work on macOS and Linux via libheif and

  with MSVC and vendored into `windows/codecs/x64` first — `doc/WINDOWS.md`
  § Image codec libraries itemizes what to build and where to put it.
